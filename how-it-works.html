The process

Five stages, start to finish.

  1. Discovery

    We talk through what your organisation does, what you're running on today, where the problems are, what you're aiming for, and who has to use the thing every day. Usually this is a conversation and a look at your current spreadsheets — no preparation needed.

  2. Blueprint

    We map your workflows, user journeys, required functionality and the information behind them. You get a written scope with a price and a timeline before any code exists, so there are no surprises later.

  3. Design

    We design the interfaces, dashboards and mobile screens to your brand. You see exactly what you're getting and we change it while changing it is easy.

  4. Build

    We develop the working product, sharing progress on a private link as we go. You're never waiting months for a big reveal that turns out to be wrong.

  5. Launch & support

    Testing, data migration from your old systems, staff training and go-live — then ongoing support as your requirements change. We don't hand over a zip file and disappear.

Common questions

Before you get in touch.

We don't have anyone technical. Is that a problem?

No — most of the organisations we work with don't. We handle the technical side and explain decisions in plain English.

How long does it take?

It depends entirely on scope, which is why we won't guess before discovery. You'll get a date in the written proposal.

What happens to our existing data?

We migrate it. Spreadsheets, exports from an old system, even paper records — bringing your history across is part of the launch stage.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. Ownership terms are set out in the proposal before you commit to anything.

Ready to talk it through?

The first conversation is free and there's no commitment attached to it.
