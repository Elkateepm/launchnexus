Built around you

No two organisations work exactly the same.

So we don't believe every organisation should be using exactly the same software. We take the time to understand how yours works before designing the technology around it.

  1. Understand

    We learn how your organisation currently operates — including the spreadsheets, the paper and the workarounds nobody mentions in the first meeting.

  2. Simplify

    We identify the unnecessary admin, the duplicated data entry and the places where information gets lost between systems.

  3. Design

    We design a system around your workflow — the screens your team will use every day, in the order they actually use them.

  4. Build

    We turn the design into a working digital product, with your feedback shaping it while changes are still cheap.

  5. Launch

    We help you introduce it into your organisation — data migrated, staff trained, and someone on the end of the phone.

  6. Improve

    Your system keeps evolving as your organisation grows. New requirements are additions, not rebuilds.

How it works

From idea to launch.

Five stages, and you know what happens at each one before we start.

  1. Discovery

    What your organisation does, what you're running on today, what's getting in the way, and who actually has to use the thing.

  2. Blueprint

    We map your workflows, user journeys and the information behind them, then agree the scope in writing before any code is written.

  3. Design

    Interfaces, dashboards and mobile screens designed to your brand. You see exactly what you're getting while it's still easy to change.

  4. Build

    We develop the working product, sharing progress on a private link so nothing is a surprise at the end.

  5. Launch & support

    Testing, data migration, training and go-live — then ongoing support as your requirements change.
