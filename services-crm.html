CRM software designed around you.

Instead of forcing your organisation into an off-the-shelf system, we learn how you work and build a CRM around it.

Possible modules

Only the parts you need.

A CRM doesn't have to mean a sales pipeline. These are the pieces we build most often — yours might use four of them, or all of them under different names.

Customer / client management

Keep contacts, history and information together in one record instead of five places.

Staff

Manage your team, their permissions and what they've done.

Projects

Track work from beginning to completion, with everyone seeing the same status.

Forms

Collect information digitally — registrations, consent, feedback, applications.

Documents

Keep important files organised and attached to the record they belong to.

Payments

Track transactions, outstanding balances and who has paid what.

Communications

Keep messages connected to the record they relate to.

Reporting

Understand what's actually happening across the organisation, without exporting to a spreadsheet first.

Automation

Reduce repetitive admin — reminders, recurring tasks and the jobs someone currently does by hand.
